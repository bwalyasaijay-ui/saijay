Food Production

Learn food preparation and production essentials with attention to good hygiene, consistency and safe working routines.

Course overview
♨

Learning through practice

Develop foundational knowledge for food-production settings through practical learning. Explore preparation, hygiene, safe handling and habits that support consistent, quality work.

Skills you can explore

  • Personal hygiene and food-safety principles
  • Preparation methods and organised workstations
  • Basic food-production processes
  • Storage, handling and cleanliness awareness
  • Teamwork and quality checks

Potential career pathways

Training may support exploration of opportunities such as:

  • Food-production assistant
  • Catering and kitchen support
  • Food-service and hospitality pathways
  • Small food-business preparation

Career pathways are examples, not a promise of employment. Outcomes depend on experience, qualifications and local opportunities.
