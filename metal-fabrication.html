Metal Fabrication

Build practical workshop skills and learn how careful planning, safe habits and precision come together in metal fabrication.

Course overview
⚒

Learning through practice

Explore workshop procedures and fabrication principles through demonstrations and supervised practical learning. Training emphasizes safe tool use and thoughtful preparation.

Skills you can explore

  • Workshop safety and protective equipment
  • Reading basic measurements and fabrication plans
  • Marking, cutting and preparing metal
  • Introduction to joining and finishing methods
  • Tool care, teamwork and quality awareness

Potential career pathways

Training may support exploration of opportunities such as:

  • Fabrication workshop assistant
  • Metalwork and production support
  • Maintenance and construction pathways
  • Small-scale fabrication and repair work

Career pathways are examples, not a promise of employment. Outcomes depend on experience, qualifications and local opportunities.
