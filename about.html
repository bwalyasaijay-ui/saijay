About the college

Skills and confidence for a changing world.

College of Skills believes access to useful skills can open new possibilities. We bring learners together to explore digital, technical and food-production training in a supportive environment.

Our focus is practical learning, thoughtful guidance and a welcoming experience for people at different stages of their learning journey.
