Frequently asked questions

Helpful starting information about courses, applications, fees and admissions.

What courses does College of Skills offer?

The courses featured here are ICT / Digital Skills, Metal Fabrication and Food Production. Contact the college to confirm current intakes and availability.

How do I apply?

Use the online application page to prepare your details. This demonstration does not send applications, so contact the college directly to complete an official application.

What are the course fees?

Fees can depend on the course and intake. Please contact the college to confirm current fees before applying.

How long do the courses take?

Schedules and course duration can vary. Ask the college for current details about the course you are interested in.

What are the entry requirements?

Entry requirements may vary by course and intake. Contact admissions for current requirements and supporting documents.

Can I get career guidance?

Career guidance is among the services described on this website. Contact the college to ask what support is currently available.

Where is the college located?

Contact the college to confirm its current campus location and directions.
