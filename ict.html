ICT / Digital Skills

Build confidence with computers, productivity tools and digital communication in guided, practical learning.

Course overview
⌘

Learning through practice

This course develops useful digital foundations for learning, work and everyday tasks. Through guided practice, learners explore how to use digital tools confidently and responsibly.

Skills you can explore

  • Computer and file-management basics
  • Word processing, spreadsheets and presentations
  • Email, online research and digital communication
  • Online safety and responsible technology use
  • An introduction to web concepts and digital projects

Potential career pathways

Training may support exploration of opportunities such as:

  • Office and administrative support
  • Digital customer-service roles
  • Entry-level IT support pathways
  • Digital tasks for small businesses

Career pathways are examples, not a promise of employment. Outcomes depend on experience, qualifications and local opportunities.
